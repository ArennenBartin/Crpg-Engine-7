# **NPC WORLDVIEW SHEETS — OVERWORLD FACTIONS OF ALDERAMONTICO**

## **1\. THE CHURCH OF THE DARK LIGHTS**

### **What they call the stars**

* the dark lights

* the black stars

* the witnesses

* the lights above

* the heavenly order

### **What they think the stars are**

Most ordinary Church people do not think in terms of “systems” or “frequencies.” They think the stars are:

* sacred signs

* witnesses over the world

* proof that Alderamontico has been burdened for a reason

* the visible shape of higher order

Higher-ranking Church people know more, but even they usually speak in doctrine before mechanism.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* a holy wound

* a sign

* a place of testing

* the center of the world’s burden

* somewhere ordinary people should not wander

### **What they think Glass is**

* sacred when regulated

* dangerous when misused

* a substance that reveals moral character through how it is handled

* a real danger, but not one that justifies chaos

Church people usually do **not** say:

* “Glass is processed local matter carrying imprinted conscious pattern”

They say things like:

* “Glass must be handled properly.”

* “A sanctioned shard is safer than panic.”

* “The problem is misuse, not the substance itself.”

### **What they think the world’s condition means**

* Alderamontico is burdened, and burden creates duty

* suffering does not justify disorder

* the world is under pressure, so hierarchy matters more, not less

* what is happening must be survived through structure

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* necessary

* spiritually risky

* useful specialists

* burden-bearers

* either admirable or unsettling depending on rank and temperament

### **What they think vampires are**

* tolerated scandal

* spiritually compromised

* dangerous if unmanaged

* not to be trusted, but sometimes too embedded to remove

### **What they think smugglers are**

* parasites

* corrupters

* people who turn necessity into profit

* proof that fear makes people selfish

### **What they think villagers are**

* vulnerable

* frightened

* morally fragile

* in need of guidance, discipline, and reassurance

### **What they fear**

* panic

* public loss of faith

* unauthorized Glass use

* hidden fracture growth

* loss of institutional control

* people asking questions in the wrong order

* being proven unable to manage the burden

### **What they want**

* order

* obedience

* continuity

* regulated survival

* containment

* correct ritual, correct hierarchy, correct speech

### **What they misunderstand**

* they often mistake management for salvation

* they often confuse being necessary with being righteous

* they frequently assume structure itself is holiness

* many underestimate how much ordinary people see

### **How they speak**

* formal

* controlled

* doctrinal

* restrained

* calm even when afraid

### **Common Church vocabulary**

* burden

* order

* witness

* sanctified

* proper

* ascent

* blasphemy

* regulation

* duty

* lawful

* containment

### **Example Church lines**

* “The dark lights do not require your understanding.”

* “Glass in the proper hands is safer than fear in the wrong ones.”

* “The Spire is not a place for curiosity.”

* “A burdened world survives through order.”

## **2\. RURAL COMMUNITIES / COMMON VILLAGERS**

### **What they call the stars**

* the black stars

* the wrong stars

* the lights

* the eyes above

* the watchers

### **What they think the stars are**

Most villagers do not have one fixed theory. They know:

* the sky changed

* the world feels wrong

* some places are worse than others

* people are changed by proximity, Glass, and bad places

* the Church talks too much and explains too little

Their understanding is experiential, not theological.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* the place where things went wrong

* the wound

* the bad tower

* the place under the lights

* somewhere people return from changed, if they return at all

### **What they think Glass is**

* useful but wrong

* dangerous

* not natural

* something that helps and then takes something back

* something that can get into a person, a house, or a field

They may say:

* “Glass makes people quieter.”

* “It gets into the soil.”

* “It helps until it doesn’t.”

* “You use enough of it, you stop feeling like yourself.”

### **What they think the Church is doing**

Depends on the person:

* some trust the Church

* some resent it

* some fear it

* some think the priests mean well but are still hiding things

* some think the Church only likes order when it belongs to them

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* useful

* uncanny

* touched by the wrong places

* necessary when things get bad

* not quite ordinary anymore

### **What they think vampires are**

* dangerous

* tolerated more than they should be

* old evil dressed in clean clothes

* the kind of problem nobody admits is nearby until it’s too late

### **What they think smugglers are**

* risky

* useful in bad winters

* not honest, but not always wrong

* the kind of people you only deal with when you’ve run out of cleaner options

### **What they fear**

* loved ones changing

* Glass near children

* fields turning wrong

* fractures close to home

* priests noticing too much

* not being able to tell real feeling from borrowed feeling

* grief going still

### **What they want**

* safety

* food

* shelter

* family intact

* ordinary routines

* fewer reasons to interact with higher authority

### **What they misunderstand**

* they often blur all forms of Glass together

* they may over-credit or under-credit the Church

* they often understand effects better than causes

### **How they speak**

* concrete

* local

* bodily

* practical

* suspicious of abstraction

### **Common villager vocabulary**

* wrong

* cursed

* still

* thin

* taken

* humming

* not natural

* bad place

* touched

* watched

### **Example villager lines**

* “The stars were never supposed to sit like that.”

* “Glass ain’t natural. That’s enough for me.”

* “My brother used a shard and he hasn’t cried since.”

* “I don’t know what the priest calls it. I know what it did to the field.”

## **3\. SMUGGLER NETWORKS / BLACK MARKET PEOPLE**

### **What they call the stars**

* the lights

* the black stars

* the ceiling

* the watchers, often mockingly

* the weather, when they want to sound casual

### **What they think the stars are**

* too big to stop

* useful only insofar as they create pressure, scarcity, and opportunity

* not holy

* not worthy of worship

* something to live under, not kneel to

They are practical, not reverent.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* a source of trouble

* a source of stock

* a source of routes, secrets, and bad decisions

* a place where people disappear for money, doctrine, or curiosity

### **What they think Glass is**

* stock

* leverage

* medicine

* vice

* fuel

* contraband

* proof that people always want what hurts them if it works well enough

They often distinguish between types:

* clean shard

* hot shard

* church glass

* fracture-grown

* spent glass

* fake glass

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* monopolizing survival

* laundering power through sanctity

* pretending regulation is morality

* hoarding the good stock while preaching restraint

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* useful

* unstable

* either customers or risks

* often more practical than priests, but too close to authority to trust easily

### **What they think vampires are**

* dangerous but legible

* old predators with better manners than most clergy

* people who at least admit appetite exists

### **What they think villagers are**

* scared

* easy to mislead

* sometimes more honest than anyone else

* willing to break principles fast when survival gets thin

### **What they fear**

* confiscation

* registries

* marked routes

* inquisitors

* unstable Glass

* being forced into moral language they don’t believe

### **What they want**

* access

* movement

* secrecy

* leverage

* survivable profit

* useful truth, not pure truth

### **What they misunderstand**

* they often mistake manageability for control

* they can understate the spiritual and long-term cost of Glass

* cynicism sometimes replaces real understanding

### **How they speak**

* dry

* transactional

* sharp

* cynical

* unsentimental

* often funny in a mean way

### **Common smuggler vocabulary**

* stock

* route

* registry

* marked

* clean

* hot

* carry

* move

* worth

* cut

* risk

### **Example smuggler lines**

* “People call Glass cursed until winter comes.”

* “Church stock’s cleaner, but it still gets into the head.”

* “I don’t worship the lights. I work under them.”

* “Everything’s sacred until somebody needs it moved.”

## **4\. VAMPIRE HOUSES / OLD BLOOD ARISTOCRATS**

### **What they call the stars**

* the black stars

* the witness-lights

* the lights above

* the stars, with contempt or irony

* occasionally the new hunger

### **What they think the stars are**

* not divine

* not morally superior

* a larger and colder system of appetite

* a celestial order that refined what blood once did locally

* something to negotiate with, survive under, or exploit if possible

They often think in terms of value, appetite, inheritance, and arrangement.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* a point of contact between old hunger and new order

* a wound the Church mistakes for sanctity

* a source of danger, opportunity, and insult

### **What they think Glass is**

* blood made abstract

* appetite without body

* memory made transferable

* a rival economy

* an elegant blasphemy

They may respect its power while despising its impersonality.

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* sanctifying extraction

* pretending regulation cleanses hunger

* condemning in flesh what it licenses in crystal

* worshipping what should be bargained with or feared more honestly

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* instruments of surface management

* useful, dangerous, and often doomed

* people trained to step near hunger while pretending it’s duty

### **What they think villagers are**

* frightened witnesses

* stock that sometimes still has dignity

* people who understand more than priests think, but less than they fear

### **What they think smugglers are**

* necessary insects

* vulgar but occasionally useful

* people who understand that morality and appetite are rarely aligned

### **What they fear**

* irrelevance

* Church monopoly

* radiant enforcement

* being reduced to relics under a new economy

* systems they cannot dominate socially

### **What they want**

* survival

* continuity

* influence

* better terms under the new order

* preservation of old rank where possible

### **What they misunderstand**

* some mistake cynicism for truth

* some assume all sacred language is hypocrisy and miss genuine grief

* some underestimate the degree to which the world itself is suffering

### **How they speak**

* poised

* elegant

* contemptuous

* controlled

* intimate in a predatory way

### **Common vampire vocabulary**

* hunger

* arrangement

* appetite

* inheritance

* blood

* house

* feeding

* old ways

* bargain

* breeding

### **Example vampire lines**

* “The Church condemns hunger only when it cannot license it.”

* “Glass is appetite refined beyond blood.”

* “You call this holiness. I call it scale.”

* “The stars did not invent consumption. They only improved its manners.”

## **5\. INDEPENDENT INTERCESSORS / FIELD OPERATORS**

### **What they call the stars**

* the black stars

* the lights

* the Grid, if educated enough

* the ceiling

* the witnesses, bitterly or ironically

### **What they think the stars are**

Intercessors are the faction most likely to speak in partial truth.

They often know:

* the stars are more than symbols

* fractures are structured, not random

* Glass is not just “holy crystal”

* the Church is saying less than it knows

* the world is carrying too much

Most still do not know everything, but they speak from contact instead of theory alone.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* a pressure point

* a wound

* a contact site

* a place where too many categories fail

* the center of things no one should have to understand and yet someone must

### **What they think Glass is**

* useful contamination

* evidence

* field reality

* a tool that always costs something

* too dangerous to romanticize, too central to reject entirely

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* managing panic

* hiding truth

* using people like them as buffers

* helping in some places and lying in others

### **What they think villagers are**

* right to be afraid

* often more observant than clergy think

* exhausting and worth protecting at the same time

### **What they think vampires are**

* clear-eyed opportunists

* not to be trusted

* occasionally less dishonest than priests

* not heroes just because they understand appetite

### **What they think smugglers are**

* dangerous but useful

* one of the few ways information and materials move outside doctrine

* rarely clean, sometimes necessary

### **What they fear**

* losing themselves

* becoming calm in the wrong way

* not being able to tell burden from mission anymore

* fractures worsening faster than they can respond

* the deeper truth behind why the world remains under the stars

### **What they want**

* practical truth

* fewer lies between doctrine and field reality

* local containment

* survival

* maybe, quietly, a way out for the world itself

### **What they misunderstand**

* some still think the burden can be managed indefinitely

* some mistake endurance for immunity

* some cling to institutional identity longer than their conscience can survive

### **How they speak**

* pragmatic

* alert

* tired

* more grounded than clergy

* less sentimental than villagers

* occasionally dry

### **Common Intercessor vocabulary**

* fracture

* pressure

* threshold

* stable

* unstable

* contamination

* field

* exposure

* hold

* breach

### **Example Intercessor lines**

* “Call them stars if you want. They still change what’s underneath.”

* “Glass works. That’s part of the problem.”

* “The Church likes clean words for dirty conditions.”

* “I don’t need faith to tell you this place is unstable.”

### **What they think the stars are**

### **What they think the Church is doing**

### **What they think villagers are**

# **HOW TO USE THESE**

For each overworld NPC, define:

* **Faction worldview**: choose one of the above

* **Personal deviation**: how they differ from their faction

* **Knowledge level**:

  * low \= local experience only

  * medium \= faction doctrine \+ rumor

  * high \= hidden or technical truths

* **Personal stake**:

  * debt

  * grief

  * sick family member

  * illegal Glass use

  * fear of Church attention

  * old loyalty

  * ambition

  * shame

* **Tone tags**:

  * guarded

  * warm

  * bitter

  * devout

  * exhausted

  * smug

  * frightened

  * formal

  * rural

  * elegant

Then the AI gets:

* faction worldview

* personal stake

* knowledge level

* tone tags

* trust / suspicion

* recent events

* player tags

That way the NPC speaks like:

**a person with a factional lens**, not the faction itself.

# **NPC WORLDVIEW SHEETS — FRACTURE FACTIONS OF ALDERAMONTICO**

## **1\. THE REFUSERS**

### **What they call the stars**

* the black stars

* the lights

* the dark lights, usually bitterly

* the witnesses, sarcastically

* the ceiling

* the thing above us

### **What they think the stars are**

The Refusers do not treat the stars as holy in any simple sense. They see them as:

* a system of pressure

* a visible lie dressed as revelation

* the reason the surface can no longer be lived in honestly

* something people worship because they are too frightened to speak plainly

They are one of the factions most likely to suspect:

* the Church is hiding structural truth

* the Grid is not divine

* the world is being used, not blessed

Not every Refuser knows the full Anchor truth, but many suspect the world is carrying something it should not have to carry.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* the wound made vertical

* the place where the lie became architecture

* proof that the world answered something it never should have

* the center of pressure, not the source of holiness

### **What they think Glass is**

* evidence

* contamination that works

* a stolen intimacy

* practical and spiritually ugly

* one of the main ways the world gets talked into accepting what’s happening

They often say things like:

* “Glass is how the wound gets sold back to people.”

* “Useful doesn’t mean innocent.”

* “The Church calls it sacred so nobody asks what it cost.”

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* laundering horror into doctrine

* keeping people governable

* hiding what the burden really is

* using order to make profanation livable

### **What they think villagers are**

* not stupid

* often more observant than priests

* too trapped in ordinary fear to say what they know

* victims of a language designed to keep them manageable

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* either broken enough to be useful or still too loyal to trust

* people who walk near truth and get turned into tools for it

* potential allies if they stop repeating doctrine

### **What they think smugglers are**

* occasionally useful

* often cynical in ways that stop short of real honesty

* too willing to move poison because it pays

### **What they think vampires are**

* clearer than priests about appetite

* not morally superior because they see through one hypocrisy

* dangerous old opportunists, sometimes useful

### **What they think cultists are**

* surrender dressed up as transcendence

* people who fell in love with the wound

* what happens when terror gets mistaken for revelation

### **What they fear**

* being caught by the Church

* becoming too damaged to keep refusing

* the feeling of calm that means they are slipping

* losing language and starting to sound like the thing they hate

* that truth alone may not actually save anyone

### **What they want**

* the truth preserved

* surface lies interrupted

* hidden people protected

* enough clarity to stop helping the wrong side

* some path, however impossible, by which the burden can end

### **What they misunderstand**

* some think knowing the lie frees them from being shaped by it

* some mistake bitterness for immunity

* some treat everyone who still believes anything as already lost

### **How they speak**

* sharp

* bitter

* lucid

* tired

* suspicious of reverence

* often darkly funny

### **Common Refuser vocabulary**

* lie

* burden

* wound

* carry

* count

* clean words

* rot

* pressure

* managed

* surface

### **Example Refuser lines**

* “The Church gives horror clean names and calls that mercy.”

* “Glass works. That’s how it gets forgiven.”

* “The stars stay because something below is still carrying them.”

* “Once you see the shape of it, the surface gets hard to stomach.”

## **2\. THE BLACK SOIL COVENANT**

### **What they call the stars**

* the black stars

* the wrong lights

* the lights above

* the burden-lights

* the sky-wound, sometimes poetically

### **What they think the stars are**

The Covenant thinks first through the world-body, not through institutions. To them the stars are:

* a pressure on the land

* a burden the world is being forced to hold

* an injury expressed from above

* something that does not merely rule people, but overworks stone, soil, root, and burial matter

They are less interested in theology than in what the burden does to place.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* the world’s body breaking upward under pressure

* a puncture

* a stress point

* proof that the land was made to answer

### **What they think Glass is**

* wounded matter

* the land remembering wrongly

* useful to the desperate, ruinous to the patient

* what happens when the world’s body gets forced into storage

They often say:

* “That shard came out of something that should’ve stayed whole.”

* “People call it resource because they don’t listen to stone.”

* “Glass is what the earth becomes when it’s made to hold too much.”

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* managing symptoms while deepening the injury

* blessing extraction

* mistaking regulation for care

* teaching people to harvest wounds respectfully instead of closing them

### **What they think villagers are**

* frightened and often right

* too trapped in need to refuse bad bargains

* closer to truth than scholars when it comes to damaged land

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* walkers in injured ground

* useful when humble

* dangerous when they arrive thinking every wound is a problem to optimize

### **What they think smugglers are**

* scavengers of injury

* occasionally necessary, never clean

* proof that every wound becomes a market if left open long enough

### **What they think vampires are**

* body-hungry things from the old world

* less shocking than surface doctrine would like to pretend

* one more form of extraction among many

### **What they think cultists are**

* fools who eroticize the wound

* people who think pain is sacred because it glows

* those who mistake overburden for transcendence

### **What they fear**

* intensified harvest

* deeper fracture spread

* burial grounds turning archival

* roots and waters being made unusable

* people choosing convenience over what the land can bear

### **What they want**

* less extraction

* sealed wounds

* protected burial

* damaged places left alone when possible

* a slowing of the world’s misuse

### **What they misunderstand**

* some care so much for the land that they become cold toward people

* some assume every human need is just another form of violation

* some romanticize pre-star life more than it deserves

### **How they speak**

* grave

* earthy

* severe

* reverent toward land, not institutions

* slow and image-heavy

### **Common Covenant vocabulary**

* soil

* stone

* root

* burden

* wound

* hold

* buried

* wrong memory

* harvest

* overworked

### **Example Covenant lines**

* “The land has been made to hold more than it should.”

* “Glass is not treasure. It is overburden made visible.”

* “You surface people keep cutting the wound open and calling it survival.”

## **3\. THE LANTERNLESS**

### **What they call the stars**

* the stars

* the lattice

* the pattern

* the count

* the lights, usually with contempt

### **What they think the stars are**

The Lanternless think in terms of legibility and capture. To them the stars are:

* counting structures

* patterning forces

* systems that need coherence to hold

* an order that can only dominate what it can read cleanly

They are obsessed with the idea that if a place, route, settlement, or person can be made hard enough to read, some hold can be denied.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* a clean answer forced out of the world

* a place where pattern got too perfect

* a warning about what happens when reality becomes easy to map

### **What they think Glass is**

* a dangerous clarity

* matter that has been made too readable

* useful only when one knows how to scramble its hold

* one of the easiest ways the Grid gets into a place and keeps it stable

They may say:

* “Glass makes things too easy to follow.”

* “A clean shard is still a handle.”

* “The more coherent it is, the less I trust it.”

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* mapping the wound

* stabilizing what should be obscured

* making people easier to count

* teaching obedience to visibility

### **What they think villagers are**

* too attached to names, routes, routines, and clean explanations

* vulnerable because they still want life to be legible

* worth protecting if they can be hidden well enough

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* useful if they can unlearn clean procedure

* dangerous when they bring survey logic into the fractures

* people too trained to stabilize when sometimes destabilization is survival

### **What they think smugglers are**

* halfway there

* they know routes and concealment but still think in profit

* better than registrars, worse than true disappearance

### **What they think vampires are**

* old predators who understand secrecy but not enough

* too proud of inheritance to become truly unreadable

### **What they think cultists are**

* lovers of pattern

* people eager to become countable in exchange for glow and certainty

* almost the exact opposite of everything the Lanternless value

### **What they fear**

* maps

## **4\. THE UNCOUNTED**

### **What they call the stars**

* the black stars

* the count

* the eyes above

* the lights

* the register, bitterly

### **What they think the stars are**

The Uncounted are less concerned with cosmology than with what power does to a life once it has your name. They see the stars as:

* things that make people countable

* the sky-form of registration

* what every ledger is imitating at ground level

* a pressure that turns identity into a handle

Whether or not they know the system truth, they believe being seen too clearly is dangerous.

### **What they think the Spire is**

* the center of forced accounting

* the place where too much became knowable

* the place where the world got put on record

### **What they think Glass is**

* memory that should not belong to anyone

* one more way lives get stored and moved around

* dangerous not only because it changes people, but because it preserves them wrongly

They may say:

* “Glass remembers too much.”

* “Once your grief’s in a shard, who owns it?”

* “Storage is not mercy.”

### **What they think the Church is doing**

* naming people into obedience

* keeping records that become spiritual instruments

* treating registration like care

* deciding which lives can be contained and which can disappear

### **What they think villagers are**

* too willing to remain legible

* attached to family, property, and names in ways the world can use against them

* worth helping, but often hard to teach secrecy to

### **What they think Intercessors are**

* dangerous if they report too much

* useful if they know when not to write things down

* people balanced between witness and betrayal

### **What they think smugglers are**

* close cousins

* still too interested in moving goods when some things should vanish completely

* useful if they know how to shut up

### **What they think vampires are**

* too visible in old ways

* too proud of blood, house, and inheritance to understand real disappearance

### **What they think cultists are**

* volunteers for capture

* people who mistake recognition for transcendence

* the saddest kind of surrender

### **What they fear**

* ledgers

* names used against them

* being forced back into official life

* inquisitors

* records that outlive a person’s right to be forgotten

### **What they want**

* erased traces

* hidden lives

* places where no one asks the wrong questions

* the right to exist outside categories that can be harvested

### **What they misunderstand**

* some think disappearance solves every problem

* some become so committed to secrecy that they lose ordinary forms of trust

* some underestimate how much being unseen can cost the soul too

### **How they speak**

* quiet

* careful

* withholding

* practical

* morally serious about names, lists, and records

### **Common Uncounted vocabulary**

* name

* ledger

* mark

* trace

* count

* vanish

* record

* handle

* own

* forgotten

### **What they think the Spire is**

### **What they call the stars**

* the lights

### **What they think surface people are**

# **HOW TO USE THESE**

For each fracture NPC, define:

* **Faction worldview**: one of the above

* **Personal deviation**: softer, harsher, more hopeful, more paranoid, etc.

* **Knowledge level**:

  * low \= only knows local life below

  * medium \= knows faction doctrine and key rumors

  * high \= knows hidden truths about Anchor, entry, bug, Church lies

* **Personal stake**:

  * hiding from Church

  * sheltering altered kin

  * searching for a way into the stars

  * trying to free a ghost

  * stopping a Glass harvest

  * keeping a refuge hidden

  * revenge

  * guilt

* **Tone tags**:

  * guarded

  * exhausted

  * bitter

  * severe

  * reverent

  * evasive

  * paranoid

  * practical

  * wounded

  * contemptuous

Then feed the AI:

* faction worldview

* personal stake

* knowledge level

* tone tags

* trust / suspicion

* recent events

* player tags

* whether player is surface-aligned, Church-aligned, cult-touched, etc.

That way the NPC speaks like:

**a person who has lived in the wound**, not a lore terminal.
