# **ALDERAMONTICO**

## **Laws of Glass Compendium**

## **Core Definition**

Glass is a Grid-born crystal that stores emotional and cognitive pattern in matter.

It does not occur naturally.

It appears only where the Grid has acted on living reality strongly enough to crystallize memory, feeling, ritual pressure, or witness into physical form.

Glass is not just a resource.

It is a recording medium, witness medium, rite medium, and contamination risk.

---

# **I. FIRST LAW**

## **Glass is not natural**

Glass does not occur on its own in the world.

It only forms where the Grid has:

* touched living beings

* affected a place intensely

* fixed strong psychic or ritual pressure into matter

* or been deliberately invoked through rite or contact

Where Glass grows, the Grid has already been there.

---

# **II. SECOND LAW**

## **Glass is not emotion made solid**

Glass is not bottled grief, fear, love, or faith.

Emotion is not the substance.

Emotion is the pattern Glass captures.

The Grid fixes strong inner states into matter, and Glass is the result.

So Glass is best understood as:

matter altered by the Grid until it can hold memory, feeling, and witness-pattern.

---

# **III. THIRD LAW**

## **Glass stores pattern**

Glass can store:

* memories

* emotional residue

* witness

* ritual states

* confession

* devotion

* terror

* shame

* obsession

* grief

* surrender

* other strong enduring inner structures

Glass stores best when the feeling or memory is:

* intense

* repeated

* ritualized

* behavior-shaping

* tied to a meaningful event or place

Passing moods imprint weakly.

Deep states imprint strongly.

---

# **IV. FOURTH LAW**

## **Glass forms only through the Grid**

No Grid, no Glass.

Even if grief, worship, violence, or ritual are present, Glass only forms when the Grid acts on that pressure and makes it materially legible.

This means the Grid is not just harvesting people. It is also:

* exposing inner states

* stabilizing them

* and turning them into usable matter

---

# **V. FIFTH LAW**

## **Glass forms in different ways**

### **1\. Event Glass**

Forms where something powerful happened:

* deaths

* rituals

* betrayals

* processions

* martyrdom

* public shame

* miracles

* failed miracles

This is often the most emotionally charged.

### **2\. Rite Glass**

Forms through deliberate sacred or technical use:

* Church rites

* witness storage

* confession

* seal work

* authorized sanctification

This is usually more stable and controlled.

### **3\. Growth Glass**

Forms gradually in places of long-term Grid pressure:

* churches

* chapels

* burial sites

* old processional roads

* underchapels

* catacombs

* repeated labor sites

* heavily processed districts

This is environmental and often normalized.

---

# **VI. SIXTH LAW**

## **Glass stores both feeling and memory**

Glass can hold:

* the emotional pattern of a place

* direct memory traces

* spoken witness

* deliberate priestly inscriptions

* remnants of events

* fragments of identity

This means Glass is used for:

* archives

* confession

* liturgy

* legal witness

* sacred preservation

* forbidden memory work

Priests can deliberately place memories into Glass.

That does not make the process clean.

---

# **VII. SEVENTH LAW**

## **Ordinary handling is usually survivable**

Glass is dangerous, but it is not so unstable that the world could not build an economy around it.

So:

* holding a shard briefly will not usually ruin you

* carrying ordinary Glass is not instant madness

* being near Glass is not automatically catastrophic

* common ritual contact is often considered acceptable

This is important.

Glass is horrifying partly because it is normalized.

People live around it.

Pray with it.

Trade it.

Build with it.

Store truth in it.

---

# **VIII. EIGHTH LAW**

## **Infection comes from intimacy, repetition, and force**

Glass becomes seriously dangerous when a person:

* handles it repeatedly

* uses it in rites

* sleeps near concentrated stores

* touches active growths

* interfaces with memory-heavy Glass

* breaks it open deliberately

* ingests it

* implants it

* channels themselves through it

* interacts with large, potent masses

So the rule is:

Glass does not deeply infect through ordinary handling.

It infects through intimacy, repetition, and force.

---

# **IX. NINTH LAW**

## **Glass transmits what it holds**

When activated or deeply engaged with, Glass can:

* bleed its stored feeling into a person

* pass on memory fragments

* distort mood

* alter perception

* impose fixations

* smooth or redirect behavior

* make someone feel foreign emotion as if it were their own

Weak Glass gives:

* moods

* flashes

* dreams

* unease

Strong Glass gives:

* memories

* compulsions

* sustained emotional contamination

* identity destabilization

* Glass-sickness

---

# **X. TENTH LAW**

## **Scale matters**

Small shards hold:

* fragments

* moods

* weak impressions

* noise

* traces

Large masses hold:

* coherent memories

* strong pattern

* stable witness

* dangerous emotional structures

* deeper contamination potential

So a tiny shard may alter dreams.

A major formation may alter a life.

---

# **XI. ELEVENTH LAW**

## **Stronger interaction means deeper consequence**

Interaction severity matters.

### **Low contact**

* brief handling

* carrying

* passing use

* proximity

Possible effects:

* slight unease

* mood tint

* stray dreams

* subtle familiarity

### **Medium contact**

* repeated use

* prolonged carrying

* ritual presence

* exposure to witness-heavy Glass

Possible effects:

* recurring thoughts

* borrowed emotion

* altered sleep

* fixation

* emotional flattening or intensification

### **High contact**

* direct rite-work

* blood contact

* implantation

* ingestion

* forced opening

* active threshold contact

* touching bleeding or awakened Glass formations

Possible effects:

* Glass-sickness

* memory invasion

* identity drift

* severe compulsion

* full psychic destabilization

* conversion risk

---

# **XII. TWELFTH LAW**

## **Glass can be awakened**

Most Glass is dormant or low-active.

Certain conditions make it more dangerous:

* blood

* ritual invocation

* Witching Hour conditions

* mass fear

* confession

* false testimony

* unresolved witness

* proximity to the Grid

* active portals or breaches

Awakened Glass:

* transmits more strongly

* records more quickly

* infects more deeply

* may reshape nearby matter

* may produce sudden conversion events

This is why some objects are safe for years until one night they are not.

---

# **XIII. THIRTEENTH LAW**

## **Glass and witness are tied together**

Glass is especially good at holding:

* testimony

* confessions

* vows

* dying words

* martyr memory

* accusation

* absolution formulas

* false witness

That makes it central to:

* religion

* law

* propaganda

* blasphemy

* investigation

Glass does not merely remember events.

It remembers how they were spiritually framed.

This is why spiritual defamation is so dangerous.

---

# **XIV. FOURTEENTH LAW**

## **Falsehood can infect Glass**

If witness is corrupted, Glass can hold:

* lies

* false accusations

* imposed guilt

* manipulated testimony

* spiritually binding untruth

This is not “just misinformation.”

A false sacred framing can have real material effect once the Grid fixes it into Glass.

This is how:

* holy objects become dangerous

* statues bleed

* accusations bind

* contact turns bodies to Glass

---

# **XV. FIFTEENTH LAW**

## **Glass is economically central**

Glass is common enough to be used.

This is part of the horror.

It appears in:

* Church rites

* relics

* seals

* confession objects

* memory storage

* infrastructure

* contraband

* noble collections

* black market tools

* investigation practices

It is regulated, categorized, traded, sanctified, and abused.

The world depends on it.

---

# **XVI. SIXTEENTH LAW**

## **Glass is morally unclean even when socially accepted**

Glass may be lawful.

Glass may be holy.

Glass may be useful.

It is still born from a process no one should fully trust.

That means:

* sanctioned does not mean pure

* sacred does not mean harmless

* normalized does not mean ethical

The Church may regulate Glass.

That does not absolve the world of needing it.

---

# **XVII. SEVENTEENTH LAW**

## **People can become Glass**

In severe cases, a person may be:

* partially taken

* fixed

* turned

* preserved as witness-bearing material

* bound into object form

* reduced to high-potency Glass state

This is rare, horrifying, and often politically buried.

Glass-turned people are not ordinary deposits.

They are unusually potent and morally catastrophic.

Something of the person remains in the pattern.

---

# **XVIII. EIGHTEENTH LAW**

## **Glass-turned people are the most powerful and most taboo form**

Glass made from a fully taken person can be used in:

* relics

* anchors

* witness cores

* sealed rites

* powerful infrastructure

* miracles

* blasphemous tools

* high Church secrecy

* black market weapons or devices

This is the darkest part of the Glass economy.

The horror is not only that people become Glass.

It is that such Glass is extremely useful.

---

# **XIX. NINETEENTH LAW**

## **Glass leaves residue after use**

Glass does not usually vanish like magic.

After strong use it may become:

* spent

* pale

* inert

* cracked

* emptied

* spiritually cold

Spent Glass still has value, but less power.

This helps the material feel real and used, not mystical and disposable.

---

# **XX. TWENTIETH LAW**

## **Glass names the world’s condition**

Common people call it “Glass” because it is:

* translucent

* reflective

* sharp

* brittle or clean-fracturing

* warm-lit from within

* like stained glass gone wrong

* like frozen tears

* like church windows infected by witness

The Grid may not call it that.

But the world does.

---

# **STANDARD GLASS CLASSES**

## **Clear Glass**

Weak imprint, ordinary use, low contamination risk.

Used in common rites, minor trade, everyday sanctioned handling.

## **Witness Glass**

Strong memory-bearing Glass.

Used in confession, archives, testimony, sacred record, investigation.

## **Veined Glass**

Mixed, unstable, emotionally tangled.

Often found in disaster sites, layered rituals, or places of contradiction.

## **Black Glass**

High machine-readability, terminal-linked, dangerous, often near core Grid activity or severe sacred corruption.

---

# **STANDARD EXPOSURE STATES**

## **Glass-Touched**

Mild influence.

Dreams, moods, faint carryover, low-level sensitivity.

## **Glass-Sick**

Noticeable alteration.

Fixation, emotional contamination, memory bleed, changed perception, instability.

## **Half-Taken**

Severe exposure.

Identity destabilization, compulsion, bodily change, conversion risk.

## **Spent**

A person drained or smoothed by repeated extraction, discharge, or deep ritual use.

Emotionally flattened, weakened, hollowed.

---

# **CHURCH DOCTRINE ON GLASS**

Officially, Glass is:

* a burdened gift

* a lawful medium of witness

* sacred when regulated

* dangerous when mishandled

* proof that reverence must outrank curiosity

The Church teaches:

* authorized use is proper

* unauthorized use is blasphemous

* overuse is hubris

* inquiry without reverence is suspect

The Church does not want Glass abolished.

It wants it controlled.

---

# **VAMPIRE VIEW OF GLASS**

Vampires understand Glass as:

* abstracted blood-value

* universalized life-pattern

* an elegant but offensive replacement for blood

* proof that the world has industrialized appetite

They may hate it, admire it, trade in it, or covet it.

# **SIMPLE DEFINITIVE SUMMARY**

Glass is a Grid-born crystal that stores memory, witness, and emotional pattern in matter. It does not occur naturally. Ordinary handling is usually survivable, but deep, repeated, or forceful interaction causes infection. It is central to religion, law, infrastructure, and horror because it is useful, normalized, and morally unclean. Wherever Glass grows, the world has already been processed.
